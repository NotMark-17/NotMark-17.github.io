Your visit

What a first appointment looks like

Nothing is started on the first visit unless you have agreed to it and you know what it costs.

We listen first

What is bothering you, how long it has been there, and any medical history or medicines you are on.

Examination, and an X-ray if needed

A look at every tooth and the gums. An X-ray only where it changes what we would advise.

Findings and options

What we found, what can be done, what happens if you wait — and an estimate of the cost and number of sittings.

You decide the pace

Urgent things first, the rest planned around your time. Nothing is booked in without your say-so.
