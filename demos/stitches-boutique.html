In the boutique

Alterations, finishing, rescues.

Not everything needs to be built from scratch. A fair amount of the work is making something you already own fit properly.

  • AlterationsTaking in, letting out, re-hemming, sleeve changes on pieces bought elsewhere.
  • Blouse & fall workBlouse stitching from a saree piece, fall and pico finishing, pre-pleating.
  • Lining & paddingCups, canvas, proper lining so a sheer fabric behaves on the day.
  • Last-minute fixesWedding-week emergencies, when the fit has gone wrong three days before.
