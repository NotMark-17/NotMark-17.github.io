Good to know

A home kitchen, not a factory

Everything is baked to order in small batches, which is the reason the notice period matters more here than at a shop counter.

  • 24 hours' notice for most cakes; custom designs and tiered cakes need longer.
  • Eggless on request — say so in the form and it's baked that way from the start.
  • Collection from Naranpura, or delivery across west Ahmedabad at a distance-based charge.
  • A small advance confirms a custom order and holds your date.
  • Festival weeks close early — Rakshabandhan and Diwali slots go first.
